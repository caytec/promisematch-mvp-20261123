Privacy

Designed for temporary copy analysis.

PromiseMatch does not require an account and does not intentionally publish your submitted copy.

What happens to inputs

Your text is processed to create the requested diagnostic. This MVP does not use a database or public report URL. Do not submit confidential, personal, medical, financial, customer, or unreleased campaign information.

AI providers

If configured by the operator, a server-side Gemini or Groq request may process the normalized input. API keys are never sent to the browser. Provider retention and terms may apply; verify them before production use.

Operational data

Requests may be rate-limited using transient server memory. Normal analytics should contain event names and operational metadata, not full user copy.

Contact

This MVP does not publish an operator support address. The deploying operator must add a verified support contact and provider-specific retention details before public launch.
