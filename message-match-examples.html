Message-match examples

Recognize the gap before your visitors do.

These short examples show how PromiseMatch separates shared promises, missing confirmations, and contradictions.

Strong match

Ad: “Automate weekly reports. Save hours every week. Start free.”

Page: “Automated weekly reporting for busy teams. Build your first report free. Start free.”

The benefit, audience context, offer, and CTA continue naturally.

CTA mismatch

Ad: “Download the guide.”

Page: “Book a sales consultation.”

The visitor expected a low-commitment download but encounters a larger commitment.

Offer contradiction

Ad: “Try it free—no credit card.”

Page: “Request pricing from our sales team.”

The page does not visibly confirm the advertised offer and may create immediate distrust.
